**Shop Management Pro+** is a high-performance, offline-first, all-in-one Enterprise ERP, Business Management, and Point of Sale (POS) solution. Designed for maximum speed, operational accuracy, and data privacy, it operates as a **100% standalone single-file web application (`index.html`)** powered by browser-native IndexedDB storage. 

Your entire business workflow—including product inventory, point-of-sale checkout, customer due ledgers, courier COD reconciliation, employee payroll, fixed asset valuations, warranty claims, damage write-offs, financial account books, and government tax ledgers—runs completely offline, privately, and locally inside your browser with zero latency or subscription costs.

## ✨ Core Enterprise Modules

### 🛒 Point of Sale (POS) & Fast Billing
- **Lightning Checkout:** Instant item lookup via hardware barcode scanner, camera scanner, or instant catalog search.
- **Category Filter Pills:** Rapidly filter items by category with visual stock badges and alert indicators.
- **Cashier Hotkeys:** Keyboard-driven checkout workflow (`F2` search, `F4` customer lookup, `F8` checkout modal).
- **Flexible Split Tenders:** Pay using Cash, Bank Transfer, Mobile Banking (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay), or Partial/Credit Due.
- **Held Carts (Suspended Sales):** Park multiple customer carts with custom notes and resume or discard them anytime.
- **Customer Combobox Sync:** Auto-calculates customer outstanding balance, due alerts, and transaction history during checkout.

### 📊 Enterprise Business Reports Hub (12 Sub-Tabs)
1. **Overview & P&L Statement:** Real-time Gross Sales Revenue, COGS, Operating Expenses, Net Profit, and Gross Margin %.
2. **Sales Report:** Filter sales invoices by date range, cashier, and payment method.
3. **Purchases & Vendor Ledger:** Supplier procurement invoices, payment history, and accounts payable.
4. **Expenses & Income Register:** Store operating overheads and secondary income streams.
5. **Customer Dues & Receivables:** Outstanding customer balances with 1-click WhatsApp due reminder generator.
6. **Product Performance Analysis:** Sales volume, gross profit per SKU, and stock turnover rates.
7. **Category & Brand Analytics:** Comparative revenue and margin performance across product categories and brands.
8. **Courier & COD Analytics:** Delivery parcel volume, collected COD funds, and shipping charges.
9. **Fixed Assets Valuation:** Asset register, purchase costs, depreciation logs, and market valuations.
10. **Damage & Loss Write-offs:** Incident logs, damaged inventory write-offs, and net financial loss.
11. **HR, Staff Payroll & Leaves:** Monthly staff base salary allocation, salary payouts, and leave register.
12. **Govt Sales Tax & VAT Ledger:** Sales tax collections and official treasury deposit challans.
- **Custom Filters & Export:** Filter by predefined ranges (Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, This Month, Last Month, This Year, Custom) and export clean CSV/Excel files with 1-click.

### 📦 Advanced Inventory, Batches & Expiry Tracking
- **Multi-Unit SKU Management:** Manage products with custom unit conversions (Pcs, Kg, Ltr, Box, Pack, Dozen, etc.).
- **Batch & Expiry Date Alerts:** Track inventory batches, expiry dates, near-expiry warning alerts, and low stock thresholds.
- **Barcode Sticker Print Studio:** Generate and print custom barcode sticky labels in grid layouts with pricing and product titles.
- **Smart Stock Reorder:** Auto-calculates reorder points based on sales velocity and generates purchase order drafts.

### 🚚 Courier Partners & COD Analytics
- **Courier Directory:** Track major courier partners (Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, Paperfly, Sundarban, SA Paribahan, etc.).
- **Shipment Tracking:** Link sales invoices to tracking numbers and parcel status (Pending, In-Transit, Delivered, Returned).
- **COD Fund Reconciliation:** Track Cash-On-Delivery collected funds vs. pending courier payouts and delivery charges.

### 🏦 Multi-Account Financial Ledger (Bank, Cash & MFS)
- **Triple-Tender Cash Flow:** Manage Physical Cash Drawer, Bank Accounts, and Mobile Financial Services (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay).
- **Deposit & Withdrawal Register:** Log inter-account transfers, bank deposits, cash withdrawals, and MFS fee deductions.
- **Daily Cash Closing:** Record opening balance, actual counted cash, system expected cash, cash variance, and closing notes.

### 👔 HR, Staff Payroll & Leave Register
- **Employee Roster:** Manage employee profiles, job titles, base salaries, contact details, and joining dates.
- **Salary Disbursement:** Process monthly staff salary payments with digital payout vouchers and cash flow logs.
- **Leave Register:** Log casual, sick, and annual leave requests with approval status tracking.

### 🛡️ Warranty Claims & Repair Center
- **Warranty Ticket System:** Log customer warranty claims with serial numbers, issue descriptions, and claim dates.
- **Claim Status Lifecycle:** Track repair stages (Received, Under Inspection, Sent to Vendor, Repaired, Replaced, Delivered).
- **Automated Customer Alerts:** Generate SMS/WhatsApp update notifications for warranty claim status.

### 🎁 Combo / Bundle Packs & Loyalty Points
- **Combo Product Builder:** Create product bundle kits (e.g., Grocery Combo, Gift Pack) that deduct component stock upon sale.
- **Customer Loyalty Points:** Award reward points per purchase amount, track point balance, and redeem points for discounts during checkout.

### ⚠️ Damage & Loss Write-off Register
- **Inventory Write-off Log:** Log broken, expired, or stolen items with cause tags and cost estimates.
- **Automated Stock Deduction:** Instantly deduct damaged quantities from inventory while recording loss in financial reports.

### 🏢 Fixed Assets & Valuation Engine
- **Asset Registration:** Record store machinery, computers, barcode scanners, POS hardware, electricals, and furniture.
- **Depreciation & Market Value:** Track initial purchase cost, accumulated depreciation, and real-time net book valuation.

### 🏛️ Govt Sales Tax & VAT Ledger
- **Automated VAT Sync:** Synchronizes invoice sales tax and generates official VAT summary statements.
- **Treasury Deposit Vouchers:** Log government tax authority treasury deposits with challan numbers and dates.

### 🔒 Security, Lock Date & Encrypted Vault
- **Master PIN Security:** Lock administrative panels, financial reports, settings, and deletion functions with a master PIN.
- **Lock Date Protection:** Prevent historical transaction editing or deletion prior to a set financial lock date.
- **Triple Backup Vault:**
  1. **Plain JSON Backup:** Human-readable full database backup.
  2. **Encrypted AES-256 Vault:** Password-protected encrypted snapshot for secure offsite storage.
  3. **Modular CSV Exports:** Export individual database tables (Products, Sales, Customers, Expenses) to spreadsheet format.
- **Recycle Bin & Data Recovery:** Soft-delete safeguard allowing easy restoration of accidentally deleted records.

## 🛠 Technical Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend Engine:** Pure Vanilla TypeScript / JavaScript with reactive DOM rendering (Zero external frontend frameworks).
- **UI & Styling:** Responsive utility styling with built-in Dark & Light themes, mobile touch optimization, and fluid layouts.
- **Persistence Layer:** Client-side IndexedDB database engine providing sub-millisecond offline read/write operations.
- **Localization Engine:** Native English 🇬🇧 and Bengali (বাংলা) 🇧🇩 translation switcher with real-time UI re-rendering.
- **Distribution Model:** Standalone Single-File Application (`index.html`). No Node.js runtime or backend server required for end users.

## 📥 Installation & Deployment

### 1. Direct Local Offline Usage (Easiest)
1. Download or clone `index.html` from the repository.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave).
3. The application is instantly ready for offline business operations.

### 2. Cloud Web Hosting
Deploy `index.html` to any web host or static provider:
- **GitHub Pages:** Enable GitHub Pages in repository settings pointing to `index.html`.
- **Netlify / Vercel:** Drop `index.html` into Netlify or Vercel for instant free web hosting.

## 📱 Mobile App Support (PWA & Android APK)

- **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Open the application URL in a mobile browser (Chrome/Safari) and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** to install it as a standalone app with offline icon launching.
- **Android APK:** Wrap `index.html` using Capacitor, WebView, or WebToApp builder to build an installable Android `.apk` file.
